I am going to UL

Support guide for the booth. School of Computing and Informatics.

The site is https://iamgoingtoul.fly.dev. The password is the same for the projector, review, and answer drafts. Get it from the professor. It is not written in this guide.

The pictures are from a practice run. Names such as Bayou Scout are nicknames the booth assigns. They are not student names.

What students do

They scan the code on the projector. The phone gives them a nickname and the questions. They do not type a name, email, or account.

One scan unlocks every question. The code stays the same all day, so it can be printed and posted. If they scan again before their time runs out, they keep the same nickname. Open /print on a signed-in laptop to print the sheet. Revoke codes retires that sheet.

Votes show up in the totals immediately. A written comment does not appear on the projector until someone approves it. Comments are limited to 140 characters.

Sign in

Before students arrive

  • On the projector laptop, open /host and sign in.
  • Press Present so the question list stays out of the way. Press Edit when you need the controls again.
  • Choose the question. Press Questions, or the number key for that question. Choosing one closes the list. Close or Escape also closes it.
  • Leave the QR code visible.
  • On a phone, open /mod. Open /ask when you want drafts. Review and Answers link to each other at the top of those pages.

The public page

The projector

  • Pause answers stops new votes. Totals and comments that are already up stay. Resume answers turns voting back on.
  • Revoke codes kills the code on the screen and on any printed sheet. Phones that already scanned keep working until their own timer ends. Print a new sheet after revoking.
  • Clear answers erases every vote, comment, and nickname. The questions stay. Do not use it to remove one line.

On the Before Tesla question, a film is cued at 5:43. Press play if the browser does not start it.

A student phone

Each person can answer a question once. After they send it, their phone shows the room totals. Ask me anything requires a written question. On the other questions, the comment is optional.

If the phone says to scan the code again, the code was revoked or the day ended. They should scan the code on the posted sheet or the projector.

Review

There is no skip. The phone stays on the oldest waiting line until you tap Approve or Reject. You cannot look at the next line first.

Reject cannot be undone. If you are unsure, reject it. A line that never appears is safer than a line you did not mean to show.

The vote still counts either way. Taking a line off the screen does not remove the vote.

A red flag means the comment has rough language or looks like a link or an email. The flag is a warning. Approve is still available, so read the line before you tap it.

If a line is already on the projector and it should not be, scroll to On the screen and tap Take off screen. The projector drops that text within a couple of seconds. Reload the review page if it was already open.

Answer drafts

A finished reply shows up under that question on every phone that has asked something. Students can leave and come back. The projector shows that question and reply after you approve it.

The reply is two or three sentences. It will not invent credits, tuition, fees, deadlines, or other official numbers. If a question needs one of those, the reply points them to louisiana.edu. Trust that over a guessed number.

New draft writes another reply and replaces it on every phone. Not on the projector yet means the question is still waiting on the Review page. The phones can already read the reply.

If something goes wrong

  • A bad comment is on the screen: Review, then Take off screen. Do not clear the whole booth.
  • You are not sure about a waiting line: Reject. You cannot bring it back.
  • The question list covers the question: Close, or Escape.
  • Phones cannot send: check whether Pause answers is on. The button will say Resume answers.
  • You cannot see what you typed for the password: Show.
  • The whole room should start over: Clear answers, and only if the professor asks for that.
